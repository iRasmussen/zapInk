# Demo recording plan

Create a real, silent-friendly demo of about 10–20 seconds. Do not use a fabricated mock-up in place of the application.

## Sequence

- Show a PowerPoint slide show.
- Write or draw a few clear marks with the pen.
- End the slide show and show PowerPoint's prompt about keeping or discarding annotations.
- Keep the annotations.
- Show the annotated presentation or its exported PDF.
- Run `zapInk` on the PowerPoint source.
- Show the same slide without the handwritten marks.

The story should make clear that the PDF preserves the live-session record while zapInk cleans the reusable PowerPoint source.

## Before publishing

- Capture the real PowerPoint for Mac workflow.
- Ensure no personal, student, workplace, or confidential information appears.
- Confirm the displayed marks are actually retained in the PDF and removed from the source copy.
- Do not claim compatibility beyond the tested Danish-language PowerPoint for Mac setup.
